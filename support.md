Need help with PhotoPile?

You can contact support at:

**[support@photopile.com](mailto:support@photopile.com)**

## Common Questions

### Does PhotoPile upload my photos?

No. PhotoPile works locally on your iPhone. It does not upload your photos or videos to a server.

### Does PhotoPile collect analytics or track users?

No. PhotoPile does not use analytics, ads, tracking, or user accounts.

### What happens when I delete photos?

PhotoPile uses Apple’s Photos system to move selected items through the normal deletion flow, such as Recently Deleted. You should review all selected items carefully before confirming.

### Can I recover deleted photos?

Recovery behavior is handled by the Photos app and iOS. Check the Recently Deleted section in the Photos app if you need to recover an item.

### Why does PhotoPile need photo library permission?

PhotoPile needs photo library access so it can show your photos, screenshots, and videos for review.

### I found a bug or have feedback. What should I do?

Send an email to **[support@photopile.com](mailto:support@photopile.com)** with a short description of the issue. If possible, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue happened.
